# Guinness Black Russian

## Ingredients

- 25ml coffee liqueur
- 25ml vodka
- 100ml cola
- 100ml stout

## Instructions

- Glassware: highball
- Ice: ice cubes
- Garnish: lime wedge

Pour coffee liqueur, vodka and cola into a tall glass with ice and stir gently to combine. Top with stout and serve with a lime wedge.
