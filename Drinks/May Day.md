# May Day

## Ingredients

- 15ml Plymouth gin
- 15ml Aperol
- 15ml freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1 barspoon simple syrup
- 3 dashes rhubarb bitters
- 60ml champagne

## Instructions

- Glassware: coupe
- Ice: none
- Garnish: none

Stir everything except the champagne with ice and strain into a chilled coupe. Top with champagne.
