# Santa on the Beach

Makes 2 to 3 servings. Adapted from [The Spruce Eats](https://www.thespruceeats.com/virgin-pina-colada-recipe-2097115).

## Ingredients

- About 250g unsweetened frozen pineapple chunks
- About 60ml ice by volume
- 180ml unsweetened pineapple juice
- 180ml unsweetened coconut milk
- Thai bird's-eye chili, starting with one thin slice
- Brown sugar, optional, to taste
- Pineapple wedges and maraschino cherries, for garnish

## Instructions

Briefly steep one thin slice of chili in the pineapple juice. Taste and remove the slice before blending. For more heat, steep it a little longer and taste again.

Blend the pineapple, ice, chili-infused juice and coconut milk until smooth. Taste and add brown sugar if needed. Pour into chilled glasses and garnish with pineapple wedges or cherries.
