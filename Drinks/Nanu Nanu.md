# Nanu Nanu

## Ingredients

- 45ml vodka
- 15ml poitín
- 9 drops absinthe
- 22.5ml grapefruit juice
- 15ml freshly squeezed lime juice
- 15ml simple syrup

## Instructions

- Glassware: old-fashioned glass
- Ice: ice cubes
- Garnish: lime wheel

Shake with ice and strain into a rocks glass over ice. Garnish with a lime wheel.
