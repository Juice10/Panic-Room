# Palomita

## Ingredients

- 80ml fresh pink grapefruit juice
- 8ml unsweetened pickle juice
- 15ml fresh lime juice
- 8ml agave syrup
- Chilled sparkling water, to top
- Grapefruit wedge

## Instructions

- Glassware: collins glass
- Ice: ice blocks
- Garnish: grapefruit wedge

Add a large handful of ice to a shaker, then add the grapefruit juice, pickle juice, lime juice and agave syrup. Shake well to chill the mocktail. Fill a tall collins glass with ice blocks and strain the Palomita mix over the ice. Add a splash of chilled sparkling water and stir gently with a bar spoon. Taste, add more sparkling water if needed, garnish with the grapefruit wedge and serve.
