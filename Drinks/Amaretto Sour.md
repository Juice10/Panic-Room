# Amaretto Sour

## Ingredients

- 45ml Disaronno
- 22.5ml cask-proof bourbon
- 15ml aquafaba
- 30ml freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 5ml rich simple syrup

## Instructions

- Glassware: old-fashioned glass
- Ice: ice blocks
- Garnish: lemon twist and 2 brandied cherries

Dry shake all ingredients, then shake again with ice. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice and garnish.
