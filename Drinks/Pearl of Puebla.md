# Pearl of Puebla

## Ingredients

- 60ml mezcal
- 22.5ml yellow Chartreuse
- 22.5ml freshly squeezed lime juice
- 4 sprigs fresh oregano
- 1 barspoon Ricard pastis
- 1 barspoon agave nectar

## Instructions

- Glassware: coupe
- Ice: none
- Garnish: none

Muddle the oregano and agave nectar. Add everything else, shake with ice and fine-strain into a chilled coupe.
